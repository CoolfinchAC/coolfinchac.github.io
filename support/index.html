CoolfinchAC support promise

Effective 1 January 2027

This is what we promise for every CoolfinchAC relay, in plain words.

Security updates for 3 years

We provide security updates free of charge for at least 3 years from the day you bought your relay. We also fix bugs that stop the relay from doing its job during that time.

How updates arrive

  • The relay checks for updates by itself and installs them at night (03:00-05:00), never in the middle of a schedule or while someone is using the AC. You can choose Ask me first or Don't check instead (app: Settings → Firmware updates).
  • Every update is digitally signed by us. The relay installs only updates with our signature, made for its model, and never an older version.
  • A new version runs on trial first. If it doesn't work properly within 10 minutes, the relay goes back to the previous version by itself.
  • Updates never erase your settings, schedules or phones.

When support ends

  • Your relay keeps working at home. Control on your home Wi-Fi and the schedules run on the relay itself and don't depend on our servers.
  • Control away from home depends on a cloud service we pay for. We will keep it running for at least the support period and for as long as we reasonably can afterwards. Before we ever switch it off, we will tell you at least 3 months in advance, by e-mail if we have your address and at coolfinchac.github.io, and explain how to keep using the relay (for example with your own message server, which the relay supports).
  • After the support period we may still publish updates, but we don't promise to.

Found a security problem?

Please tell us at CoolfinchAC@gmail.com. We will confirm within 5 working days, keep you informed, fix confirmed problems as fast as we can, and credit you if you wish. Please give us reasonable time to fix a problem before you publish it. We report actively exploited vulnerabilities to the authorities as EU law requires, and we tell customers when they need to act.

Warranty

This promise comes on top of your legal rights. In the EU you have at least a 2-year legal guarantee when goods are faulty. Our warranty terms: coolfinchac.github.io.

How it works

Set up in a few minutes with your phone. No app store, no account: the CoolfinchAC app is a web app you add to your Home Screen.

  1. Plug it in next to the wall controller

    In a wall socket in the same room, within about 3 metres of the controller, with the USB power adapter from the box.

  2. Scan the two codes on the card

    Code 1 joins the relay's own Wi-Fi, code 2 opens setup. Type your home Wi-Fi password, then confirm the same number on your phone and on the wall controller to pair them.

  3. Open the app

    Tap Open the app and add it to your Home Screen. It works on your home Wi-Fi and anywhere with internet. Add the other phones in your home with a QR code.

Privacy and security

Your home's data stays in your home. Here is what that means, in plain words.

  • No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no cookies. The app keeps its key in your phone's own storage.
  • Your data stays on the relay: schedules, the 7-day history, the phones you added. We can't see it. A factory reset erases it.
  • Control from away is encrypted end to end. A message service (EMQX Cloud, in the EU) passes the messages but can't read them or send commands.
  • Only the phones you add can control the AC, each with its own key. Unused invitation codes expire after 15 minutes.
  • Signed updates: the relay installs only software signed by us, made for it, and never an older version.
  • What we do see: the message service sees which relay and phone talk and when, and their internet addresses. The app's files come from Cloudflare, which sees your phone's address like any website.
  • A small health report every 15 minutes (software version, Wi-Fi signal, memory, restarts) helps us spot problems. No temperatures, no schedules, no names.

Details: privacy notice. Found a security problem? Tell us.

Compatibility and requirements

Check these before you order.

Your air conditioner

  • Works with Daikin BRC1H (Madoka) wall controllers (they connect over Bluetooth).
  • One wall controller per relay. Other controllers and other brands are not supported.
  • A wall socket within about 3 metres of the controller, where your Wi-Fi reaches.

Your home and phone

  • 2.4 GHz home Wi-Fi with internet (almost every router has it).
  • iPhone with Safari. Notifications need iOS 16.4 or later and the app opened from the Home Screen.
  • Android phone with Chrome.
  • Daikin's own Madoka app still works: release Bluetooth for 10 minutes from CoolfinchAC when you need it.

Questions

If yours isn't here, write to us.

Does it replace the wall controller?

No. The relay pairs with your wall controller over Bluetooth, like Daikin's app does. The controller stays on the wall and works exactly as before; whatever you change there shows up in the app, and the other way round. Nothing in the air conditioner is rewired or modified.

What if the internet is down?

At home, the app offers Open the home Wi-Fi page, which controls the AC directly through your home Wi-Fi. Schedules and timers run on the relay and keep running. After a power cut the relay needs the internet once to set its clock before schedules run again. Control from away comes back by itself when the internet does.

What if CoolfinchAC stops one day?

Your relay keeps working at home: control on your home Wi-Fi and the schedules run on the relay and don't depend on our servers. We promise free security updates for at least 3 years from purchase, and before we ever switch off control from away, we tell you at least 3 months in advance and explain how to keep using it (the relay can also use your own message server). See the support promise.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No. You pay once. There is no account to create, no password to remember, and nothing to install from an app store. Your phone gets its own key when you set up or when a family member adds you with a QR code.

Who can control my air conditioner?

Only the phones you add. Each phone has its own key, and you can remove any phone at once (for example a lost one). Guests get limited access until a date you choose. Commands from away are encrypted end to end, so neither the message service nor we can read them or send commands.

Can I still use Daikin's Madoka app?

Yes. The wall controller talks to one app at a time, so CoolfinchAC can release Bluetooth for 10 minutes (Settings → Use Daikin's Madoka app). It reconnects by itself afterwards.

I got a new router or changed the Wi-Fi password. Now what?

Unplug and plug in the relay three times in a row. It opens its own Wi-Fi for 10 minutes; scan the two codes on the card again and choose the new network. Nothing is erased: schedules, phones and history stay.
